  <div style="text-align: justify;">
    HPT is a cognitively motivated evaluation framework for large language models that categorizes prompting strategies by their cognitive demands and introduces the Hierarchical Prompting Index (HPI) to quantify task complexity. The framework enables systematic analysis of reasoning capabilities across datasets and models, yielding consistent performance improvements and interpretable insights into LLM problem-solving behavior.<br>
